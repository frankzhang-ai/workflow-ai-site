Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Clear expectations matter when workflows touch client communications and information.

Who is this for?

Small professional-services businesses in the Greater Toronto Area, especially insurance brokerages, real estate firms, and accounting or consulting practices. Collaboration can also happen remotely.

What happens on the free 15-minute call?

We’ll hear how a recurring process works today, what is taking time or causing follow-up to slip, and whether it makes sense to explore it further. There is no obligation.

How does pricing work?

The engagement model is Setup + Monthly. If there is interest, a paid Workflow Audit maps the process and opportunities; a written proposal follows. Software subscription and API usage costs are paid by the client.

How do you approach privacy?

We take a PIPEDA-minded approach: use information with consent or other lawful authority, be transparent about how it is used, and minimize the data involved. Third-party AI processors may be used; any processors and data handling will be explained and agreed with the client.

Will a person review sensitive information?

Yes. Financial, insurance, and identity data is not left solely to automation. A human reviews it.

What do you not automate?

We do not provide automated underwriting, investment advice, or automated high-impact personal decisions of that kind. Workflows are for administrative processes, email classification, reminders, document preparation, and customer-service support.

Where do you work?

We serve the Greater Toronto Area, including Milton, Oakville, and Mississauga. Remote collaboration is also possible; there is no client-facing office location to visit.
